# Code of Conduct

🇫🇷 [Version française](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming space, people who take part in this project —
through an issue, a discussion, or any other repo-related exchange — pledge to make it a
respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, or
viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Show respect for differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Be patient — this project is maintained by a single person, in their spare time.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (address, email...) without explicit consent.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or harassing behavior may be reported to the maintainer (see
[docs/en/SUPPORT.md](./SUPPORT.md)). All complaints will be reviewed and responded to as deemed
necessary and appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces (issues, discussions) and in public when an
individual is representing the project.
